# Rules notes

Where each rule used in the first match lives in the official rules. Later milestones add a short summary, in our own words, beside each item as it is implemented. Rulebook text is never pasted here.

- **Rules version targeted:** 1.0.2
- **Source:** Trench Crusade Digital Rulebook 1.0.2, the free PDF on the publisher's rules page (https://www.trenchcrusade.com/rules/)
- **Re-check due:** when version 1.1 is published (announced for late November 2026)

## How these references were gathered

The section names and page numbers come from the rulebook's table of contents, read with an automated tool on 14 October 2026. They have not been checked against a printed or downloaded copy by a person. Before a milestone implements an item, confirm its pages against your own copy of the 1.0.2 PDF and tick the "Confirmed" column.

The rulebook has a short "Core Rules" chapter (pages 14 to 21) and a longer "Comprehensive Rules" chapter (pages 22 to 51). The comprehensive chapter is the primary reference; core-rules pages are listed as secondary where they cover the same topic.
